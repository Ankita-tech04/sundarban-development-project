About Us

Sundarban Development Initiative is a grassroots organization committed to the social and economic betterment of the Sundarban region. We aim to drive change by addressing key challenges such as climate change, livelihood improvement, education, and community-based tourism. Our team works in close partnership with local stakeholders to implement projects that respect the rich cultural heritage and biodiversity of the region. Through education, advocacy, and collaboration, we aim to create a sustainable and resilient community, ensuring a brighter future for the people of Sundarban.
